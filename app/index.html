Yaw (needs a tilted wheel)

With a tilted wheel, vertical spin can be moved into the wheel: I_z·γ̇ + I_w·sin(tilt)·ω stays constant. A slow outer loop sets the wheel-speed target, and the balance loop tracks it. Try tilt 10° in Plant with pivot friction off, apply, tick this, then tap sideways (T): the spin stops and the wheel keeps the momentum. With friction on, friction stops the spin first, and the wheel can't hand momentum back to turn the body, so heading control is limited. Works best at 5–15° tilt. Above ~20° balancing suffers.

Plant (physics)

End mass, beam and CoM are model-mismatch tests: the controller still assumes the paper's values. Wheel tilt is a design change, and the controller is redesigned for it. Paper values: tilt 0, unbalance 0.
